Serhii Pohorilyi

Doctor of Science in Engineering (D.Sc.), Professor, Laureate of the State Prize of Ukraine in Science and Technology

General information

Research areas:

  • schematology of parallel algorithms for supercomputer computing and the study of their implementations in cluster computing (based on the mathematical apparatus of modified Glushkov systems of algorithmic algebras and the Real-Time Process Algebra (RTPA))
  • application of genetic algorithms in high-performance computer systems
  • architectures of homogeneous and heterogeneous cluster systems and methods of organising cluster computing
  • Computational linguistics

Teaching experience

For more than 25 years at Kyiv University he has been lecturing to students on the following courses:

  • discrete mathematics
  • design methodology of open computer systems
  • programming for UNIX platforms

For many years he taught the courses:

  • software construction
  • modern operating systems
  • Internet application development

Scientific and organisational work

He actively supervises four PhD students and is building a scientific school. Over the last three years, five Candidate of Sciences dissertations have been defended under his supervision and one has been accepted for defence (specialities 01.05.03 and 05.13.05).

He serves on the editorial boards of the international scientific journals “Applied and Computational Mathematics” (ISSN 1683-3511) and “Proceedings of the Institute of Applied Mathematics” (ISSN 2225-0530), and is a member of the editorial boards of the scientific journals “Bulletin of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv” (Series: Physics and Mathematics) and “Scientific Papers of DonNTU” (Series: Informatics, Cybernetics and Computer Engineering). He has published more than 220 scientific works, including 6 monographs, 3 textbooks and 11 study guides.

Over the last five years he has been a member of the programme committees of the international conferences COIA 2011, COIA 2013, TAAPSD 2011, TAAPSD 2012, TAAPSD 2013, etc. He has given talks at seven international scientific conferences outside Ukraine.

He carries out extensive scientific and organisational work: he serves on the Expert Council on Informatics and Instrument Engineering of the State Accreditation Commission of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine; on the Specialised Academic Council for the defence of doctoral dissertations D 26.001.09 (Faculty of Cybernetics, Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv); and is the Academic Secretary of the Comprehensive Research Programme “Informatisation of Society” of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv.

He has repeatedly completed internships at leading computer centres and holds 11 international certificates.
