The 2026/2027 winter examination session will be held remotely

12 November 2026 · Teaching

All news

The winter examination session of the 2026/2027 academic year at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv will be held remotely, using information and communication technologies, for students of all years and all forms of study. Semester assessment will run from 23 November to 15 December 2026. The procedure is set by Rector’s Order No. 1275-32 of 11 November 2026 “On holding the winter examination session of the 2026/2027 academic year”. All forms of semester assessment will take place online: credit tests, examinations and defences of term papers and projects.

The order repeals clause 3.2 of Order No. 1234-32 of 30 October 2026 and Order No. 1262-32 of 6 November 2026 “On the specifics of holding examinations in the 2026/2027 academic year”.

Main requirements for examinations:

  • the examination schedule is approved and communicated to students and teachers before the session begins; at the consultation before the examination, the teacher explains the procedure, assessment criteria, platform and means of communication, academic integrity rules, results of continuous assessment and the list of students not admitted to the examination;
  • every examination must include an oral part in the form of an interview based on the course syllabus; if a written paper or a test is planned, the interview takes place after it;
  • a student is given at least 30 minutes to prepare an answer in an oral examination; for written or written-and-oral examinations, no more than three hours; the total duration of an examination for an academic group does not exceed six hours;
  • during the examination, the student stays on a video call with the camera and microphone on; before the examination starts, the examiner identifies the student by their student ID or another identity document shown over the video call; photographing or recording documents and storing images of them is not allowed;
  • written answers are handwritten or prepared electronically and sent to the examiner’s email address or uploaded to the learning platform chosen by the examiner;
  • the examination may be recorded (audio and video) at the examiner’s decision, with participants notified in advance;
  • technical failures (power cuts, network outages) cannot be a reason for an “unsatisfactory” grade; an interruption must be reported to the teacher, the group leader or the dean’s office (directorate) within the same day, after which the examination can be continued at another time agreed with the administration, including together with the next group; such a continuation is not considered a retake;
  • academic integrity requirements remain mandatory: if a violation is established (use of unauthorised sources, help from third parties, deliberately turning off the camera or microphone), the examiner may remove the student from the examination with an “unsatisfactory” grade.

Students and teachers who do not have adequate conditions or technical means to take part in examinations will be able to take and conduct them online in the University’s academic buildings (classrooms, department rooms, shelters, “Invincibility Points”) by prior arrangement with the relevant faculty. By 20 November 2026, deans’ offices will set the list of such rooms and the procedure for booking them.

Clarifications to the order (No. 4453-I of 12 November 2026):

  • the format is the same for all units, regardless of the year and form of study; the order does not allow a faculty, institute or department to switch to an in-person or blended format;
  • for credit tests scheduled for 16–22 November or earlier, the approved timetable and the relevant University acts apply; deans’ offices are advised to hold credit tests online where possible, and a student who cannot attend an in-person credit test may agree remote participation or another time with the dean’s office; such an absence is not a reason for non-admission or a lower grade;
  • the format of qualification thesis defences and qualification examinations is set by a separate instruction of the dean or director;
  • one device with a camera and microphone is enough for the video call, a second camera is not required; to verify identity, it is enough to briefly show a document to the camera, sending a photo or a scan is not required;
  • preparation time is not multiplied by the number of students: students can prepare at the same time and answer one by one;
  • even if the student and the teacher are in the same building, the examination takes place online on the chosen platform, for example Zoom or Google Meet;
  • a student who disagrees with a grade can say so to the examiner on the day it is announced and, if necessary, submit a reasoned application to the dean or director.
