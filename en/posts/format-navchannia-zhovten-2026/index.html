CH 0115 October 2026

Study format from 17 October to 1 November 2026

In accordance with Order No. 1165-32 of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv dated 14 October 2026, the study format for the period from 17 October to 1 November 2026 inclusive has been set as follows:

  • classes are mostly held remotely according to the timetable;
  • faculties, institutes and colleges may hold classes in university buildings; during an air-raid alert, such classes may be continued in the shelter of the respective building;
  • in-person classes are announced at least three calendar days in advance; students and lecturers receive the timetable and information about shelters by corporate email.

On 29 October, because of the unified state qualification examination in public administration and civil service, no classes will be scheduled in the university buildings, including at the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems.

The previous decision on remote work was in effect from 29 September to 16 October 2026.
