In English

Pavlo Biletskyi, PhD in Computer Engineering — researcher and lecturer at the Department of Computer Engineering, Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems, Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv.

He leads a research group working on long-term memory architectures for large language models and on the evaluation of LLMs in Ukrainian, and is the principal investigator of the project “Development of a scalable long-term memory architecture for large language models to process ultra-long contexts”, submitted to the national young-researcher competition of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine (proposed term 2027–2028; under review, decision expected by the end of 2026).

Research interests: natural language processing, coreference resolution in Ukrainian texts, large language models, long-context architectures, GPU-accelerated computing. Eight publications, three of them indexed in Scopus.

He has supervised seven bachelor’s theses in 2025–2026, on LLM benchmarking for Ukrainian, training corpora for LLM memory systems, LLM-based assistants for office software, agentic systems for financial data analysis, and video-streaming infrastructure. See the research group page for details.
