Andrii Kriuchyn

Doctor of Science in Engineering (D.Sc.), Professor, Deputy Director of the Institute for Information Recording of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, Corresponding Member of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, Laureate of the State Prize of Ukraine in Science and Technology

tel.: (044) 454-21-52, 0678934052

General information

Research areas:

  • information technologies, methods and systems of information recording
  • scientometrics
  • technologies for preserving cultural heritage

Teaching experience-28 years.

He delivers the lecture courses:

  • external devices of the latest computer systems
