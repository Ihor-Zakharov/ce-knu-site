Full application process and documents

Features of the 2026 admission campaign

Persons who have completed full general secondary education are admitted to the first year of the University to study for the Bachelor’s degree. The licensed enrolment for the Bachelor’s degree on the basis of full general secondary education (FGSE) in speciality F7 “Computer Engineering” is 100 persons.

Weighting coefficients of the competitive NMT subjects - Ukrainian Language (minimum score 101, weighting coefficient 0.3) - Mathematics (minimum score 101, weighting coefficient 0.5) - History of Ukraine (minimum score 101, weighting coefficient 0.2)

Procedure for submitting applications and documents

Applicants on the basis of full general secondary education (FGSE) to speciality F7 “Computer Engineering” submit applications electronically, except in the cases specified by the “Admission Rules”.

To take part in the competitive selection, applicants may submit up to five applications for state-funded places in fixed and open competitive offers, and up to twenty applications for non-budget competitive offers.

An electronic application is submitted by the applicant by filling in an online electronic form and is considered by the University Admissions Committee in the manner prescribed by law.

A paper application is submitted by the applicant in person to the Admissions Committee. The details of each paper application are registered in the USEDE by an authorised person of the Admissions Committee on the day the application is accepted.

In the application, applicants indicate the competitive offer, specifying the speciality, educational programme and mode of study.

To take part in the competition for admission to different modes of study, applicants submit separate applications.

Each application states its priority; a priority value of 1 (one) means the highest priority. The priority of applications indicated by the applicant cannot be changed.

The ranking list of applicants is ordered:

  • by competitive score, from highest to lowest;
  • by application priority, from first to last;
  • by the results of the review of motivation letters.

Persons who have applied for admission to the University and take part in the competitive selection for state-funded places, after the Admissions Committee decides to recommend them for enrolment, must, within the established deadlines, fulfil the requirements for enrolment in state-funded places, namely submit in person to the Admissions (Selection) Committee the following documents:

  1. a copy of the identity document (one of the documents) provided for by the Law of Ukraine “On the Unified State Demographic Register and Documents Confirming Ukrainian Citizenship, Certifying a Person’s Identity or Special Status” (passport or ID card);
  2. a copy of the military registration document (for persons liable for military service, a military ID or a temporary certificate of a person liable for military service; for conscripts, a certificate of registration at a conscription office);
  3. a copy of the taxpayer registration card number (identification code);
  4. the state-issued document on the previously obtained educational degree (educational and qualification level) on the basis of which admission is carried out, if information about it is not stored in the Unified State Electronic Database on Education;
  5. 4 colour photographs, 3 × 4 cm;
  6. the applicant’s consent to the processing of personal data;
  7. a white paper binder folder (for forming the applicant’s personal file).

Copies of documents are not accepted without presentation of the originals.

It is also necessary to conclude a study agreement between the University and the applicant.

In addition to submission in person, the originals of the documents required for enrolment may be submitted by sending their scanned copies, with the applicant’s qualified electronic signature applied to the respective files, to the email address of the selection committee of the faculty to which the applicant has been recommended for enrolment, within the established deadlines. If the applicant does not provide the original documents within twenty calendar days after the start of studies, the enrolment order is cancelled in respect of the enrolment of that person.

Note: full admission information can be found in the “Rules of Admission to Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv in 2026” (in Ukrainian)

Life at KNU beyond lectures

What additional opportunities do students of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv have

  1. Out-of-town students are provided with a dormitory
  2. On the territory of the student campus there is the University Clinic of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv, which provides timely medical care to students and staff of the University.
  3. A canteen where both students and teachers of the University eat. It is located in the neighbouring building, connected by an internal passage, and has four spacious halls.
  4. The University also offers the opportunity to obtain an additional military speciality at the Military Institute. Two years of study at the military department make it possible to obtain the rank of reserve officer in a military speciality.
  5. For physical education classes and sports, the University has a sports building with halls, a swimming pool and a stadium.
  6. The University Student Parliament gives young people the opportunity both to socialise with their peers and to try their hand at active public life. In addition, there are vibrant parties, quests, sports competitions, beauty contests and social events.
