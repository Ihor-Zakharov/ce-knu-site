CH 0114 December 2014

A monograph and a study guide published

.

The monograph “Cluster Computing Methods”, prepared by a team of lecturers of the Department of Computer Engineering (S. D. Pohorilyi, Yu. V. Boiko, R. I. Levchenko, V. A. Marianovskyi), and the study guide “Programming Numerical Methods in Python”, approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine (A. Yu. Doroshenko, S. D. Pohorilyi, Ya. Yu. Dorohyi, Ye. V. Hlushko; edited by A. V. Anisimov, Corresponding Member of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine), have been published.

Published
14 December 2014
