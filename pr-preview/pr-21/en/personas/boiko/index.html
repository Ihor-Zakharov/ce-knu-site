Yurii Boiko

PhD in Physics and Mathematics (Candidate of Sciences), Associate Professor, Head of the University Computing Centre (ICC), Head of the Department of Computer Engineering of the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems, Laureate of the State Prize of Ukraine in Science and Technology

General information

In 1984 he graduated from the Faculty of Radiophysics of Taras Shevchenko Kyiv State University. Speciality “Radiophysics and Electronics”, qualification “Radiophysicist, Research Engineer”.

After completing compulsory military service in officer positions in the Armed Forces, in 1986 he began working at the University as an Engineer of the Research Department. From 1988 to 1992 he was a PhD student at the Department of Semiconductor Electronics. He worked as a Senior Engineer and Head of Sector of the Scientific and Methodological Centre for Training and Retraining of Engineering Personnel of Taras Shevchenko University of Kyiv. Since 1999 he has served as Head of the University Computing Centre (ICC).

Main stages of his teaching career: from 1993, Assistant Professor at the Department of Radio Electronics; from 2000, Associate Professor at the Department of Semiconductor Electronics; from 2006 to 2012, Head of the Department of Semiconductor Electronics (Department of Computer Engineering since 2010); from 2012, Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Engineering. In 2021 he was elected Head of the Department of Computer Engineering.

He teaches the courses “Fundamentals of Computer Hardware and Software” (1st year, 1st semester, Bachelor’s programme), “Publishing Systems” (1st year, 1st semester, Master’s programme), “Computer Circuit Design” (2nd year, 3rd semester, Bachelor’s programme), “Interfaces of Data Exchange Systems” (4th year, 8th semester, Bachelor’s programme), “Fundamentals of Information Technologies IT Essentials” (1st year, PhD programme).

He has 90 publications, including 1 patent, 2 copyright certificates for a work, 2 monographs, a textbook (approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine), a study guide (approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine), several electronic teaching publications, and a number of IT training programmes for schools approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine), including 11 publications in the Scopus database, h-index 6.

Since 1986 he has been the principal investigator of a number of research projects in semiconductor electronics and semiconductor physics. Since 2000 he has led a number of research projects in information technologies.

Under his supervision, 4 dissertations for the degree of Candidate of Technical Sciences have been defended:

  1. Andrii Oleksandrovych Salnikov. “Methods and tools for integrating virtual organisations into grid infrastructures”, 05.13.05 – computer systems and components, Candidate of Technical Sciences.
  2. Yevhen Andriiovych Sliusar. “Methods for ensuring the functional integrity of grid infrastructures”, 05.13.05 – computer systems and components, Candidate of Technical Sciences.
  3. Kostiantyn Serhiiovych Dieiev, “Investigation of network interaction using a deep packet inspection system”, 05.13.05 - computer systems and components, Candidate of Technical Sciences.
  4. Oleksandr Frantsovych Boretskyi, “Methods for integrating virtualisation technologies into the grid”, 01.05.03 - mathematical support and software of computers and systems, Candidate of Technical Sciences.
