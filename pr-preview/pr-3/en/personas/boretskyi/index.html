Oleksandr Boretskyi

Assistant Professor

Lissajous figure · 4:5

tel.: (044) 526-12-14

General information

Since 2010 he has worked at the University’s Computing Centre (ICC). Since 2012 he has been an instructor at the Cisco Networking Academy established on the basis of the Computing Centre (ICC) of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv. He graduated from Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv in 2013. He teaches and conducts laboratory sessions in the courses: “Hardware and Software”, “Parallel and Distributed Computing”, “Telecommunication Technologies”, “Databases for Network Infrastructures”, “Parallel and Distributed Computing”.

Research areas:

  • high-performance computing environments
  • distributed computing infrastructures
  • grid systems
