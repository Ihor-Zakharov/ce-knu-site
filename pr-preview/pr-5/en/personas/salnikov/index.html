Lissajous figure · 5:4

General information

Research areas:

  • high-performance and high-availability systems
  • distributed computing, Grid and Cloud systems

Teaching experience

Since 2008 he has been teaching specialised professional training courses in networking technologies (Cisco Certified Instructor Trainer). Since 2011 he has been teaching specialised professional training courses (HP Certified Instructor) on UNIX-family operating systems. Since 2013 he has been teaching courses on networking technologies and building Internet services at the Department of Computer Engineering.
