CH 0115 March 2024

An authorised FortiNet academy has opened.

.

On 15 March 2024, an authorised FortiNet academy was opened at the Department of Computer Engineering of the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems. Cooperation with FortiNet opens up new opportunities for the department’s students to study network security.

Fortinet is one of the world’s leading developers of cybersecurity solutions. As part of the cooperation, students will be trained at the authorised academy and will gain access to up-to-date learning materials on Next-Generation Firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems (IPS/IDS), solutions for protecting web resources and email services, and FortiNet products. In addition to theoretical training, students will have the opportunity to acquire practical skills in working with solutions for protecting and controlling computer networks during laboratory work.

Cooperation with leading manufacturers of cybersecurity solutions makes it possible to prepare students to work with modern computer systems that are widely used by the world’s leading companies.
