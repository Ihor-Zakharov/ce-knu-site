Congratulations to the winners!!

The Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems congratulates the team “Synthesis by Candlelight” (“Syntez pry Svichkakh”) on winning the jubilee 20th All-Ukrainian Students’ Physicists’ Tournament, held on 3-7 February 2023, and wishes the team members inspiration, creative success and an exciting game at the 15th International Physicists’ Tournament https://2023.iptnet.info/ in Paris.
