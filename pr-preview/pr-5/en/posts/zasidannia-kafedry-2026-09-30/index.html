Department meeting on 30 September 2026

On 30 September 2026, a meeting of the Department of Computer Engineering was held, chaired by the Head of the Department, Associate Professor Yu. V. Boiko. The meeting took place online.

The following issues were discussed at the meeting:

  • the results of the meeting of the Scientific and Methodological Council — information from Associate Professor Ye. A. Sliusar;
  • orders on practical training — report by Associate Professor S. P. Zahorodniuk;
  • the specifics of the department staff’s work in the near future — report by Associate Professor Yu. V. Boiko.
