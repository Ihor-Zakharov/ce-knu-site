Employers

The department’s full letter to employers

Dear employers!

Graduates of our department who study in the speciality “Computer Engineering” traditionally demonstrate broad knowledge in the field of information technology and are able to apply various methods and mechanisms that can be used to expand and develop your enterprise. The graduates’ knowledge can be classified as follows:

  • Programming languages: knowledge of and proficiency in one or more programming languages, including C++, Python, C#, JScript.
  • Software development: knowledge of and experience with widely used libraries and frameworks applied in software development, in particular Django, Entity Framework, JQuery, .NET Framework.
  • Database management: use of database management systems, including MSSQL, MySQL and LDAP.
  • Network technologies: knowledge of the principles, fundamentals and component base for building local and wide area computer networks, including technologies from Cisco, Huawei, Fortinet, MikroTik.
  • Cybersecurity: knowledge and understanding of security threats and ways of protecting against them, including authorisation protocols, identity recognition systems, and information protection methods.
  • Cloud computing: understanding of cloud computing platforms and services, including AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, as well as Grid technologies.
  • Artificial intelligence, machine learning and expert systems: knowledge of artificial intelligence concepts and algorithms, as well as experience with specialised tools, including TensorFlow, PyTorch.
  • Design of complex digital circuits and microprocessors using the Proteus computer-aided design system, as well as using field-programmable gate arrays (FPGA) and the hardware description languages VHDL/System Verilog.
  • Data analysis and visualisation: the ability to analyse large volumes of data and to create visualisations of programs, services and operating systems.
  • User experience (UX) and user interface (UI) design: understanding of UX and UI design principles, as well as experience with specialised tools, including Sketch, Adobe XD, WordPress and Figma.
  • Project management: understanding of project management methodologies and tools, including Agile, DevOps, SAP, Scrum and Kanban.
  • Communication: extensive practice in written and oral communication, including the ability to explain technical concepts to any interested parties at the scientific, engineering and technical, and popular consulting levels alike.

If you have any questions, suggestions, recommendations or initiatives regarding the content of the education that students of the Department of Computer Engineering receive throughout their entire period of study, please send them to the email address ce.frecs@knu.ua
