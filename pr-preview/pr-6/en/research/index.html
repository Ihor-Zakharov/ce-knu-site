Research

High-performance computing, digital communications and artificial intelligence.

Scientific activity

The main areas of research at the department are:

Department researchers conduct leading fundamental and applied studies at the intersection of hardware platforms, supercomputing, and artificial intelligence.

⚡

High-Performance & Distributed Computing

The use of modern IT technologies to create high-performance computing and distributed systems, grid environments and cloud platforms.

📡

Digital Communications & Protocols

Novel methods for creating and studying digital communication systems, signal transmission optimization and specialized telecommunication networks.

🖥️

Supercomputing & Process Algebra

Application of novel methods and approaches to the creation and analysis of supercomputer systems, mathematical apparatus of modified systems of algorithmic algebras, real-time process algebra, genetic algorithms, workflow patterns, and coloured Petri nets.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Neural networks, large language models (LLM), computational linguistics and natural language processing (NLP), computer vision and digital image processing.

National recognition

State Awards and Prizes

The significant contributions of department scientists to national and global science have been recognized with Ukraine’s highest state accolades.

🥇 State Prize of Ukraine 2018

State Prize of Ukraine in Science and Technology (2018)

Laureates: Prof. S. D. Pohorilyi, Assoc. Prof. Yu. V. Boiko

For participation in research "Methods and Novel Approaches to the Design, Management and Application of High-Performance IT Infrastructures".

Main result: development of a coherent, scientifically grounded methodology for supporting the processes of designing, managing and using IT infrastructure.

🎖️ Presidential Prize 2018

President of Ukraine’s Prize for Young Scientists (2018)

Laureates: Assist. Profs. A. O. Salnikov, Ye. A. Sliusar

For participation in research "Creation of a High-Performance Grid Infrastructure for Studying Conformational Changes of Biopolymers".

Main result: creation of an integrated information and computing platform, which has significantly increased the efficiency of computations in computer studies of biological macromolecules and their complexes.

Flagship developments

The department’s main research projects

01

The first computing cluster (supercomputer) in Ukraine was created and put into operation to solve problems in fundamental and applied sciences requiring large amounts of machine time and processing big data.

02

The first segment of Ukrainian Academic Grid resources was created and populated, uniting 5 computing clusters of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv and NAS of Ukraine institutes.

03

Using the mathematical apparatus of algorithmic algebra systems, schematology was developed for parallel versions of widely used routing algorithms (RIP, OSPF, etc.) in enterprise networks, intranets, and the Internet.

04

Tools were created for modelling and performance evaluation of parallel and distributed algorithms in high-performance environments.

Global scientific reach

International Scientific Conferences & Cooperation

Both lecturers and students of the Department of Computer Engineering take an active part in international scientific conferences.

At Baku University (Azerbaijan), on 27–29 August 2015, the 5th International Conference COIA 2015 (Control and Optimization with Industrial Applications) took place, where Professor S. D. Pohorilyi presented a paper on combinatorial optimization methods.

Since 2000 to the present, Professor V. A. Lvov has been collaborating with the University of the Balearic Islands (Spain). At the invitation of the UIB Governing Board, he has made 7 visits as a full visiting professor.

Department researchers, PhDs in Technical Sciences A. O. Salnikov and Ye. A. Sliusar take part in annual NorduGrid international conferences and technical meetings on developing the Advanced Resource Connector (ARC) middleware, presenting results from the Ukrainian National Grid.

Department faculty regularly contribute to specialized national and international forums on secure networks and critical communication systems.

Specialised research group

Long-term memory and evaluation of large language models

Group lead: Pavlo Biletskyi, PhD in Computer Engineering. The group studies scalable external memory architectures for LLMs to process ultra-long contexts and develops Ukrainian-language evaluation benchmarks.
