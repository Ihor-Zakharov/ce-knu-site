On 5 February 2024, the national stage of the "ICT Competition" took place, with students of Ukrainian universities taking part. The participants were students of Kyiv Polytechnic Institute, Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv and the State University of Information and Communication Technologies.

Our university's team was represented by students of the Department of Computer Engineering: Danylo Hrytskyi (3rd year), Serhii Savenko (4th year) and Bohdan Myronov (3rd year). All the participants were trained at the department's Huawei Networking Academy under the supervision of Vitalii Anatoliiovych Marianovskyi, PhD in Engineering (Candidate of Technical Sciences). The department's staff wish the participants success in the next stages of the competition!
