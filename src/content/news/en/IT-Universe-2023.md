We are pleased to announce the official opening of the new season of the International Student IT Olympiad IT-Universe, "IT-Universe-2023", and invite you to take part. Registration of higher education institutions for IT-Universe opened on 1 March. Student registration opened on 15 March. The competition programme is still being finalised, but 12 contests have already been announced: come in, have a look and choose the ones you would like to take part in (you may take part in the first stage of several contests).

We hope that this year we will finally manage to hold an in-person final. But we already have a "plan B" and the experience of a "remote final", so we are ready for that option too :). We look forward to seeing you! Join us! Together we will create the IT Universe!

More about the start of IT-Universe: <https://it-universe.org/blog/news/news_IT_universe.php?ELEMENT_ID=8183>
