On 20 March 2024, a discussion will be held of the educational and professional programme and the educational and scientific programme (EPP and ESP) for the training of higher education students in speciality 123 "Computer Engineering". A wide range of issues related to filling the EPP and ESP with substantive thematic courses is put forward for discussion. In particular, it is planned to identify the main groups of professionally oriented courses and the cycles of related courses containing logically connected and interdependent teaching materials. Each course is to be assigned to the appropriate cycle or group, possibly with its title changed or extended, or the existing course may be replaced entirely by another one.

In addition, it is planned to review the topics of all lectures, practical classes and laboratory work in every course in order to ensure the most effective combination of fundamental classical models, methods, algorithms and approaches with modern, globally widespread mechanisms, solutions and technical tools.

All interested and competent persons are invited to the discussion of the EPP and ESP educational programmes:

At 16:00 - meeting with employers and stakeholders.

At 17:30 - meeting with alumni and students.
