In accordance with Order No. 1095-32 of the First Vice-Rector of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv dated 28 September 2026, due to the deterioration of the security situation in Kyiv, university staff have been transferred to remote work from 29 September to 16 October 2026 inclusive.

For this period:

- the educational process is carried out using distance learning technologies in accordance with the approved timetable;
- during an air-raid alert, classes are suspended while participants in the educational process move to a shelter, and missing classes for this reason is not a violation;
- matters concerning laboratory classes, practical training and assessments are decided by the heads of academic units, taking into account the security situation.

If the security situation changes, the period of validity of the order may be changed by a separate order.
