The Huawei ICT Competition is an annual global event for students and teachers of colleges and universities. It has become an international platform for competition and exchange of experience that enables participants to strengthen their ICT knowledge, improve their practical skills and foster innovation using the latest technologies and platforms. In China, the competition is included in the list of national competitions for university students, and worldwide it is recognised as a key partner flagship programme of the UNESCO Global Skills Academy.

Two teams from Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv advanced to the global stage of the competition in the **Networking** and **Computing** tracks. Only five teams from the European region (out of five regions in total) were selected for the global stage, and only the best team in each track could progress further.

Our university's students took a prize-winning **third place** in both tracks, Networking and Computing.

Perseverance, initiative and hard work during preparation for the competition played a key role in these results. Also important were the integration of modern information technologies into the teaching process at the Department of Computer Engineering and the university's cooperation with employers and manufacturers in the field of information technologies.

More about the competition: <https://www.huawei.com/minisite/ict-competition-2025-2026-global/en/index.html>
