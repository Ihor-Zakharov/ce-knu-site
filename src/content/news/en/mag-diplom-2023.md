On 14 June 2023, the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems held a ceremony to award diplomas to its Master's graduates. The event took place in an exceptionally solemn, yet warm and moving atmosphere. Heartfelt wishes to the faculty's graduates were expressed by the Vice-Rector for Research Hanna Tolstanova, the Dean of the Faculty Andrii Netreba, the Heads of Departments Ihor Anisimov, Yurii Boiko, Serhii Radchenko, Volodymyr Vysotskyi and Hanna Karlash, the Deputy Deans Nataliia Bashmakova, Yuliia Veremii and Nataliia Horbovtsova, and the faculty's lecturers. Dear graduates! This day is important because it symbolises your achievements and marks the successful completion of your studies. Your work and accomplishments will become the foundation for the future development of the country. We are proud of you! The diploma you have received is a recognition of your painstaking work and your readiness for the challenges that await you in the future. Opportunities to reach new heights and develop further professionally are open to you. Our warmest congratulations!
