In the 2nd semester of the 2022/2023 academic year, the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems plans to use a blended format for organising the educational process in accordance with Rector's Order No. 795-32 (<https://www.univ.kiev.ua/news/12584>) of 27.12.2022. From 23 January to 3 February 2023, classes will be held remotely using information and communication technologies.
