The Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems invites students on 21 February 2022 at 10:30 to the open lecture "Core Tools of a Big Data Engineer" by Dmytro Minochkin (mentor at SoftServe IT Academy). The lecture aims to acquaint the audience with the nature of the work and the current state of organising data collection, processing and storage and of decision-making, and to select and form a group of participants for advanced training and work in this field.
