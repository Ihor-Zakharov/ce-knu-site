![](../../../assets/images/news/1610-huawei-01.jpg "On 28 October, a Memorandum on expanding cooperation was signed in the Main (Red) Building of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv (photo by Andrii Yelovikov)")

It was signed at a formal ceremony by the Rector of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv and the Director of **Huawei Ukraine**, Zhou Haoze, in the presence of the Deputy Minister of Education and Science of Ukraine, Maksym Strikha, and representatives of the university administration and students.

Rector Leonid Huberskyi noted the exceptional productivity of the cooperation with **Huawei**, which began 4 years ago at the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems. He thanked the partners for their consistency and for their high appraisal of the business contacts and their outcomes, and expressed the hope that this fruitful cooperation would continue.

The Director of "Huawei Ukraine", Zhou Haoze, in turn expressed satisfaction with the way the cooperation had been organised and with its outcomes, which gave confidence that it could continue and raised expectations of even more significant results. They also provided grounds for signing the Memorandum on its expansion.

Before the official signing, the KNU Confucius Institute held a "China Day" of sorts in the Red Building. Those interested could take part in master classes in calligraphy, painting, Chinese knot tying and paper cutting, as well as in a tea ceremony. An exhibition about the company and its products was also set up, with a separate stand devoted to smartphones and smartwatches. At the end of the ceremony, the representatives of both parties cut a celebratory cake, as is traditional.
