Students, please note the information on the organisation of the educational process for students of the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems in the 2023-24 academic year.

In accordance with the academic calendar for first- and second-level higher education programmes of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv for the 2023/2024 academic year (<https://knu.ua/ua/news/12769>):

Form of organisation of teaching and semester assessment for students of the faculty in the 1st semester of the 2023-24 academic year.

1st- and 2nd-year students of all educational programmes at the Bachelor's level:

1)  in-person learning: 01.09.2023–30.09.2023 and 20.11.23–31.12.2023;
2)  blended format: 01.10.2023–19.11.2023.

3rd- and 4th-year students of all educational programmes at the Bachelor's level:

1)  distance learning: 01.09.2023–30.09.2023;
2)  in-person learning: 01.10.23–05.11.2023 and 11.12.2023–31.12.2023;
3)  blended learning: 06.11.2023–10.12.2023.

1st-year students of all educational programmes at the Master's level:

1)  in-person learning: 01.10.2023–05.11.2023 and 11.12.2023–31.12.2023;
2)  blended format: 06.11.2023–10.12.2023.

2nd-year students of all educational programmes at the Master's level:

1)  in-person learning: 11.12.2023–31.12.2023;
2)  blended format: 01.09.2023–10.12.2023.
