The textbook presents the essentials of the tools and methods of programming in Python, a general-purpose, interpreted, object-oriented, high-level programming language that is developed as open source on many platforms and is available free of charge for public use.

The [textbook](/files/1603-book-phyton.pdf) is intended for students of higher education institutions in the fields of study "Informatics", "Applied Mathematics", "Software Engineering", "Computer Engineering" and "Systems Engineering", as well as for researchers, engineers, PhD students and everyone interested in the rapid development of scientific application software.
