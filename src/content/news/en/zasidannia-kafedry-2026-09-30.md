On 30 September 2026, a meeting of the Department of Computer Engineering was held, chaired by the Head of the Department, Associate Professor [Yu. V. Boiko](/personas/boiko/). The meeting took place online.

The following issues were discussed at the meeting:

- the results of the meeting of the Scientific and Methodological Council — information from Associate Professor [Ye. A. Sliusar](/personas/sliusar/);
- orders on practical training — report by Associate Professor [S. P. Zahorodniuk](/personas/zahorodniuk/);
- the specifics of the department staff's work in the near future — report by Associate Professor Yu. V. Boiko.
