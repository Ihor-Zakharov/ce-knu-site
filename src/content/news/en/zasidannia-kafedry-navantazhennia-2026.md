On 24 September 2026, a meeting of the Department of Computer Engineering was held, the main item of which was the distribution of the lecturers' teaching load for the 2026–2027 academic year.

The draft teaching load, prepared in advance and refined several times based on the lecturers' suggestions, covers all classroom and non-classroom load. It meets all the standards for classroom hours.

Among the main changes discussed at the meeting:

- the updates to the courses "Computer Systems Design Technologies" and "Microprocessor Engineering" have been taken into account;
- laboratory work in "Software Engineering" is now conducted by the department's own staff;
- in the second year, under the new educational programme, laboratory classes will be held in two groups without division by track;
- all second-year PhD students have been assigned teaching load for their assistant teaching practice.
