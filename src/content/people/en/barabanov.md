## General information

Born on 9 February 1970 in Kyiv. In 1993 he graduated from the Faculty of Problems of Physics and Energy of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. After graduation he began working at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv, first as an Engineer and, from 1996, as an Assistant Professor. In 1999 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation "Quantum-mechanical theory of spin-dependent recombination in semiconductors" in the speciality "Physics of Semiconductors and Dielectrics". Since 2002 he has worked as an Associate Professor at the Department of Semiconductor Electronics of the Faculty of Radiophysics.

His research interests include theoretical studies of spin-dependent phenomena in semiconductors and near-field effects in nanosystems.

He delivers the lecture courses "Mathematical Processing of Experimental Data", "Computer-Aided Design of Computer Systems", "Digital Signal Processing" and "Computer-Aided Design Systems in Radio Electronics". He conducts practical classes and laboratory sessions for students of the Department of Computer Engineering. He is a co-author of the quantum-mechanical theory of spin-dependent recombination in semiconductors.

Author of more than forty scientific works, including two monographs and one textbook.
