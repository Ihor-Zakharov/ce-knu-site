[Oleksandr Bauzha](/personas/bauzha)

*PhD in Physics and Mathematics (Candidate of Sciences), Associate Professor*

## General information

Born on 8 January 1976 in Kyiv. In 1999 he graduated from the Faculty of Radiophysics of Taras Shevchenko Kyiv State University. In 2010 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation. His research interests are quantum mechanics, digital circuit design, computer logic and artificial neural networks.

He delivers the lecture courses:

- Nanophysics and Nanotechnologies
- Quantum Computing
- Peripheral Devices
- Computer Logic
- Applied Theory of Digital Automata

He conducts practical classes and laboratory sessions for students of the Department of Computer Engineering.

Author of more than 50 scientific works and 6 teaching and methodological works. Most important scientific works:

1.  O. Voskoboynikov, O. Bauga, C. P. Lee, and O. Tretyak, “Magnetic properties of parabolic quantum dots in the presence of the spin-orbit interaction,” Journal of Applied Physics, vol. 94, no. 9, pp. 5891–5895, 2003, doi: 10.1063/1.1614426.
2.  O. S. Bauzha, “Effect of spin-orbit interaction on the magneto-optical spectra of InSb quantum dots,” Journal of Nano- and Electronic Physics, vol. 6, no. 4, 2014.
3.  B. Sus, N. Tmienova, I. Revenchuk, O. Bauzha, and S. Stirenko, “Gamification approach to the creation of virtual laboratory works and educational courses,” in CEUR Workshop Proceedings, 2020, vol. 2711, pp. 68–78.
4.  T. Chaikivskyi, B. Sus, S. Zagorodnyuk, O. Bauzha, and V. Lytvyn, “Modeling of Epoxidation Process by the Means of Artificial Neural Network,” in Proceedings of the 6th International Conference on Computational Linguistics and Intelligent Systems (COLINS 2022).
