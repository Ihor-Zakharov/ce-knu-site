## General information

Research areas:

- natural language processing (NLP), coreference resolution in Ukrainian-language texts
- application of large language models (LLM)
- use of graphics processing units to accelerate computations

He conducts laboratory sessions in the courses:

- Programming for UNIX Systems
- Digital Signal Processing
- Applied Theory of Digital Automata
- Database Organisation

and seminars in the course "Interfaces of Data Exchange Systems".
