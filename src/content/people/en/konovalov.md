## General information

Born in 1974, he graduated from the Faculty of Radiophysics of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv in 1996. Since 1999 he has worked as an Assistant Professor at the Department of Semiconductor Electronics, which in 2010 was renamed the Department of Computer Engineering. In 2005 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation "Components of characteristic energy loss spectra of electrons reflected by Al, In and Ge films" in speciality 01.04.04 "Physical Electronics". In 2006–2016 he also held part-time administrative positions at the University's Scientific and Methodological Centre for the Organisation of the Educational Process (head of sector, deputy director).

## Research

His current research is devoted to machine learning technologies, in particular the development of predictive classification and regression models for various applications based on artificial neural networks of modern architectures, support vector machines, decision trees, random forests, the nearest neighbours method, etc., as well as on the ensemble approach. Recent research topics include: "Machine recognition of diseases from chest X-ray images", "Recognition of cuneiform signs using a graph convolutional network", "Machine recognition of a person's age from an image of their face"

## Teaching experience

He currently delivers the lecture courses "Data Analysis in Python" (Bachelor's programme), "Artificial Intelligence Technologies" (Master's programme), "Evolutionary Computation" (Master's programme), "Computational Intelligence" (PhD programme). In previous years he delivered the lecture courses "Computer Experiment", "Symbolic Mathematics Systems", "Methods of Investigating Semiconductor Materials and Structures".
