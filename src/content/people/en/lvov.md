## General information

Research areas:

- **physical properties of shape memory alloys**
- **physics of magnetically ordered media**

## Teaching experience

For many years he delivered lectures in algebra and differential equations. He lectures in mathematical analysis. In different years he has lectured in the physics of magnetic phenomena, statistical physics and semiconductor physics.
