## General information

He graduated from Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv in 2006. In 2006–2009 he was a PhD student at the Department of Semiconductor Electronics. In 2010 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation "Creation and investigation of flexible homogeneous architectures of cluster systems".

He teaches and conducts laboratory sessions in the courses:

- "Computer Networks",
- "Switching and Routing in Computer Networks",
- "Routing in Computer Networks",
- "Security of Computer Networks and Systems".

While studying at the Faculty of Radiophysics he was fascinated by the principles of how networks work. To confirm the experience gained, he completed a number of certifications at the Computing Centre of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv and obtained more than twenty different certificates, including:

Cisco Systems:

- Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP)
- CCNA Security
- CCNA Wireless

Hewlett-Packard

- HP ASE – Network Infrastructure (2011)
- HP ASE – Wireless Networks Implementer V1

Huawei

- Huawei Certified Datacom Professional – HCDP Enterprise IP
