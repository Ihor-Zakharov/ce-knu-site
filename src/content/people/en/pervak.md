## General information

Research areas:

- **thin film physics**
- **photonic crystals**
- **laser physics**
- **synthesis of multilayer structures**
- **optical properties of nanostructures**
- **optical spectroscopy of crystals**

Teaching experience

For many years he has been lecturing on semiconductor electronics, optoelectronics, and the optics of multilayer coatings. In different years he lectured on surface physics, current problems of electronics, and semiconductor technology.
