## General information

Born on 9 April 1963 in Kyiv. In 1991 he graduated from the Faculty of Informatics and Computer Engineering of the Kyiv Polytechnic Institute. He worked at the "Arsenal Plant" production association. Since 2015 he has been working at Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv, at the Faculty of Radiophysics, Electronics and Computer Systems, as a software engineer.
