## General information

Born in 1962, Head of the Teaching Laboratory. He has been working at the Faculty of Radiophysics since 1978. He worked as a technician and an engineer at the Department of Semiconductor Physics in the research group of Yu. V. Vorobiov.

After graduating from the evening division of the faculty in 1985, he worked as a first-category engineer in the semiconductor physics laboratory practicum. Since 2003 he has been Head of the Teaching Laboratory of the Department of Semiconductor Electronics, and since 2010, Head of the Teaching Laboratory of the Department of Computer Engineering.

Co-author of the teaching guide "Laboratory Practicum in Semiconductor Physics" for students of the Faculty of Radiophysics.
